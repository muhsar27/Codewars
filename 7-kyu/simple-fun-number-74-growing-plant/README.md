<h2><a href=https://www.codewars.com/kata/58941fec8afa3618c9000184/train/go target="_blank">Simple Fun #74: Growing Plant</a></h2><h3>7 kyu</h3><h3 id="task">Task</h3><p> Each day a plant is growing by <code>upSpeed</code> meters. Each night that plant's height decreases by <code>downSpeed</code> meters due to the lack of sun heat. Initially, plant is 0 meters tall. We plant the seed at the beginning of a day. We want to know when the height of the plant will reach a certain level.</p><h3 id="example">Example</h3><p> For <code>upSpeed = 100, downSpeed = 10 and desiredHeight = 910</code>, the output should be <code>10</code>.</p><pre><code>After day 1 --&gt; 100After night 1 --&gt; 90After day 2 --&gt; 190After night 2 --&gt; 180After day 3 --&gt; 280After night 3 --&gt; 270After day 4 --&gt; 370After night 4 --&gt; 360After day 5 --&gt; 460After night 5 --&gt; 450After day 6 --&gt; 550After night 6 --&gt; 540After day 7 --&gt; 640After night 7 --&gt; 630After day 8 --&gt; 730After night 8 --&gt; 720After day 9 --&gt; 820After night 9 --&gt; 810After day 10 --&gt; 910 </code></pre><p> For <code>upSpeed = 10, downSpeed = 9 and desiredHeight = 4</code>, the output should be <code>1</code>.</p><p> Because the plant reach to the desired height at day 1(10 meters).</p><pre><code>After day 1 --&gt; 10</code></pre><h3 id="inputoutput">Input/Output</h3><ul><li><p><code>[input]</code> integer <code>upSpeed</code></p><p> A positive integer representing the daily growth.</p><p> Constraints: <code>5 ≤ upSpeed ≤ 100.</code></p></li><li><p><code>[input]</code> integer <code>downSpeed</code></p><p> A positive integer representing the nightly decline.</p><p> Constraints: <code>2 ≤ downSpeed &lt; upSpeed.</code></p></li><li><p><code>[input]</code> integer <code>desiredHeight</code></p><p> A positive integer representing the threshold.</p><p> Constraints: <code>4 ≤ desiredHeight ≤ 1000.</code></p></li><li><p><code>[output]</code> an integer</p><p> The number of days that it will take for the plant to reach/pass desiredHeight (including the last day in the total count).</p></li></ul>
